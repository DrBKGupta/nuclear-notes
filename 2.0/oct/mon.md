Lung carcinomas are broadly divided into two major histological categories: **Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)** (~85% of cases) and **Small Cell Lung Cancer (SCLC)** (~15% of cases).

---

### **1. Non-Small Cell Lung Carcinoma (NSCLC)**

#### **A. Adenocarcinoma**
* **Prevalence & Location:** Most common histologic type of primary bronchogenic carcinoma overall. Typically presents as a **peripheral lesion**.
* **Associations:** Most common lung cancer in non-smokers and women. Strongly associated with **pleural effusion**.
* **Subtype / Variant:** Formerly termed *Bronchioalveolar Carcinoma (BAC)*, presenting as a solitary pulmonary nodule or ground-glass opacity.

#### **B. Squamous Cell Carcinoma (SCC)**
* **Location & Presentation:** Classically located **centrally** near the hilar region.
* **High-Yield Features:**
  * Most common lung carcinoma associated with **cavitation**.
  * Most common histologic type associated with **Pancoast tumors (superior sulcus tumors)**.
  * **Histology:** Keratin pearls and intercellular bridges.
  * **Paraneoplastic Effect:** Produces **Parathyroid Hormone-related Peptide (PTHrP)**, leading to hypercalcemia.

#### **C. Large Cell Carcinoma**
* **Features:** Poorly differentiated, highly anaplastic neuroendocrine or non-neuroendocrine malignancy.
* **Location:** Can be central or peripheral, typically showing rapid growth and early metastasis.

---

### **2. Small Cell Lung Carcinoma (SCLC)**

* **Cellular Origin:** Derived from **Kulchitsky cells** (neuroendocrine argentaffin cells along the bronchial epithelium).
* **Location & Behavior:** Located **centrally**. Extremely aggressive, rapidly growing, and early to metastasize.
* **Histology:** Small blue cells with scant cytoplasm ("oat cells") and a high mitotic rate. Positive for neuroendocrine markers: **Chromogranin A, Synaptophysin, and Neuron-Specific Enolase (NSE)**.
* **Paraneoplastic Syndromes:** Highly associated with ectopic hormone production and autoimmune paraneoplastic conditions:
  * **SIADH** (Inappropriate ADH/AVP secretion).
  * **Cushing Syndrome** (Ectopic ACTH secretion).
  * **Lambert-Eaton Myasthenic Syndrome:** Autoantibodies against presynaptic voltage-gated calcium channels.

---

### **3. Neuroendocrine & Other Primary Lung Tumors**

* **Bronchial Carcinoid Tumor:** Low-grade neuroendocrine tumor arising from **Kulchitsky cells**. Can secrete serotonin (5-HT), leading to **Carcinoid Syndrome** (flushing, diarrhea, wheezing, right-sided heart valvular lesions) if metastasized or systemic.
* **Hamartoma:** The **most common benign tumor of the lung**, often containing disorganized cartilage, fat, and connective tissue.

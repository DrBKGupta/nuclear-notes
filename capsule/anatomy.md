### brain stem lesion

**Clinical stem / question**

> How to classify and differentiate brain stem stroke syndromes (Rule of 4s, Midbrain, Pons, Medulla).

**Core concept / trap**

> Rule of 4s &amp; Brainstem Level: Midbrain (CN 3,4), Pons (CN 5,6,7,8), Medulla (CN 9,10,11,12).
>  Medial Structures (M): Motor tract, Medial lemniscus, MLF, Motor CN nuclei (3,4,6,12).
>  Lateral Structures (S): Spinothalamic tract, Spinocerebellar/ICP, Sympathetic chain (Horner's), Sensory Trigeminal (CN V).
>
>  Weber's Syndrome (Medial Midbrain): CN III palsy + contralateral hemiplegia (PCA/Basilar branch).
>  Benedikt's Syndrome (Paramedian Midbrain): CN III palsy + contralateral ataxia &amp; tremor (Red nucleus).
>
>  Foville's Syndrome (Dorsolateral Lower Pons): Horizontal gaze palsy + LMN facial palsy + contralateral hemiplegia.
>  Millard-Gubler Syndrome (Ventral Lower Pons): CN VI weakness + facial palsy + contralateral hemiplegia.
>
>  Wallenberg Syndrome (Lateral Medulla via PICA): Dysphagia/hoarseness (Nucleus ambiguus), crossed sensory loss (face ipsi, body contra), ipsilateral limb ataxia, Horner's. Note: No motor weakness!
>  Dejerine's Syndrome (Medial Medulla via ASA): Ipsilateral tongue deviation (CN XII), contralateral proprioception loss, contralateral hemiparesis sparing face.

- **Confidence:** Med
- **Frequency:** Not sure

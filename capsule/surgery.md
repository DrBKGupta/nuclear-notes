### pediatric surgery

**Clinical stem / question**

> patent urachus

**Core concept / trap**

> so in the embryological life the umbilicus and the bladder are connected to each other by the URACHUS  before  birth it got change into the Median Umbilical ligament but in the some infant it does not occurs and cause the patent URACHUS and from this the urine is comes out form the umbilicus so the confirmatory diagnosis is the urine is coming out from the umbilicus and the uric acid and the creatinine is high in the umbilicus fluid which are coming from the umbilicus 
> Patent Urachus: The entire urachal canal remains completely open, maintaining a direct connection between the bladder and the umbilicus.
> Urachal Sinus: The urachus remains open at the umbilical end but is closed at the bladder end, forming a blind-ending tract.
> Urachal Cyst: Both the umbilical and bladder ends close, but a segment in the middle remains open and fluid-filled, creating a cystic mass.
> Urachal Diverticulum: The urachus remains open at the bladder end while closing at the umbilical end, forming a pouch off the bladder dome.

- **Confidence:** Med
- **Frequency:** Not sure

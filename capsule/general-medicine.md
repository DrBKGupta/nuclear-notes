**Clinical stem / question**

> Von Recklinghausen's Disease (NF1) and Bilateral Acoustic Neuromas (NF2) clinical criteria and genetics.

**Core concept / trap**

> it is a dominant autosomal genetic disorder which usually effects the skin and Nervous system it is usually two type 
> NF1 is the mutation on the 17q11.2 NF1 gene which codes the neurofibromin protein which is a tumor suppresser gene which done the downregulation of the RAS oncogene pathway.
> NF1 has the criterion in which the two is needed for the disease 
> six or more cafe-au-lait macules over 5mm before puberty over 15  mm after puberty 
>  axillary or inguinal freckling
> two or more neurofibromas of any type, or one plexiform neurofibroma
> optic pathway glioma
> two or  more lisch nodules, or two or more choroidal abnormalities.
>  Distinctive bone lesion; sphenoid dysplasia, anterolateral tibial bowing, or long-bone pseudarthrosis.
>  Heterozygous pathogenic NF1 variant with an allele fraction of 50% in normal tissue . and A pathogenic NF1 variant(50% allele fraction) is a criterion since 2021 but only one
